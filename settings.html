Pay Now on invoices

Choose whether your business offers Paystack online checkout on invoices. This is a business-wide setting for invoices, not a per-invoice choice. When switched off, customers can still use manual payment instructions where available.

Paystack transaction fees

Paystack charges transaction fees when online payments are processed. Fees vary by payment method. Depending on your Paystack fee settings, these may be deducted from your settlement or added at checkout. ValoraTap does not set a single fee amount.
