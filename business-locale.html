Regional settings

Country, region & currency.

Tell ValoraTap where your business operates. Your default currency will follow the country you select and will be used as the starting currency for invoices and financial records.

This sets the ISO country code used by VT.
The default currency is stored on the business profile and copied onto new invoices.
How VT uses this
Invoices

New invoices start in your default business currency.

Receipts

Payment records retain the currency in which the transaction occurred.

VT Pay

Payment providers are checked separately for supported settlement currencies.
